SHELFTALK COMMUNITY

Community Guidelines

We want ShelfTalk to be welcoming to readers and authors everywhere.

Be constructive

Reviews and discussions should focus on books, ideas and experiences. Disagreement is welcome; harassment is not.

Respect rights

Do not post copyrighted books, private information, impersonation, spam or material you do not have permission to share.

Keep the platform useful

No scams, malicious links, abusive behavior or misleading author/book information.
